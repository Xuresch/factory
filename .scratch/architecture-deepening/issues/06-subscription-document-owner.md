## Triage

**Verified:**

- Python's `subscriptions()` assembles the standalone JSON from configuration, local job documents and an outside scan. `Outside.scan()` calls `summarize()` to deduplicate and price local session usage. The reporter's `scan_outside()` already reuses that Python scan. Rust's `Hub::subscriptions()` assembles the same response shape from stored plans and jobs, and `merge_outside()` combines machine summaries. The response assembly is duplicated across runtimes. The claim that Rust repeats Python's transcript accounting does not hold.
- `outside_statements()` stores both the scan document and projected monthly rows. `merge_outside()` sums the documents from ready scans, while `Hub::overview()` independently sums ready scans' projected months and deduplicates subscriptions again. `planPage()` computes monthly job and fee totals in the browser. Neither the existing merged document nor the projected rows supply one comparison that both views consume.
- A throwaway fixture used the running hub's two JSON responses and executed the current monthly calculation extracted from `planPage()` in Node with the time zone set to UTC. A $50 job with a $20 Claude split and $10 of ready outside usage yielded $60 in the overview and $30 in Plans. `project()` retains the $30 residual as `other`. `Hub::subscriptions()` exports only the Claude and Codex splits, and `planPage()` totals only those agents.
- The fixture also confirmed that the hub response drops a plan's `since` field. `subscription_statements()` stores the name, agent and monthly price, while standalone `subscriptions()` passes through `since` and `planPage()` uses it when computing fees. The overview tile compares against the full monthly Plan fee and uses a monthly daily mean for Monthly pace. Plans presents the elapsed Plan fee and uses a recent job window for Monthly pace.

**Recommendation:** ready-for-agent. The fixture reproduces the wrong API-equivalent in Plans, and the maintainer has settled the remaining design decisions. Ticket 01 supplies the Outside usage month keys and remains the prerequisite.

## Decisions

- **2026-11-20** One Rust module in the hub owns the monthly comparison for overview and Plans. Standalone keeps its Python path through a compatibility adapter. Standalone parity is out of scope.
- **2026-11-20** Hub months are keyed in UTC. Use the Outside usage month keys supplied by ticket 01.
- **2026-11-20** Reports carry the plan's `since` into the hub. Older records without it count as active. Both views clearly label the full monthly Plan fee and the elapsed Plan fee.
- **2026-11-20** Use one Monthly pace wherever it is shown. Use the last-seven-day mean for factory jobs and the current-month mean for Outside usage. Require at least three days of data.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug

**Summary:** Have the hub overview and Plans use the same monthly comparison, including factory cost attributed to `other`.

**Current behavior:**

`Hub::overview()` sums jobs and ready Outside usage for the current and previous months and counts deduplicated plan fees. `Hub::subscriptions()` exposes job and scan data, and `planPage()` computes its own comparison with browser-local month keys. Plans drops the `other` share, so the same data can show $60 API-equivalent on the overview and $30 on Plans. The hub drops a reported plan's `since` month, and the two views use different fee and Monthly pace calculations.

**Desired behavior:**

- For a given snapshot and UTC clock, one Rust module provides the monthly comparison that both hub views display. Its monthly rows include API-equivalent, the factory and Outside usage amounts and their Agent splits, the full and elapsed Plan fee, and Monthly pace when available. Readers may select rows for display or format the values.
- Assign factory jobs by their start month in UTC and include retained jobs. Use the glossary's Agent split rules, including `other` when present and the Claude fallback for legacy total-only jobs. Include Outside usage only from ready scans and use month keys from 01 as supplied.
- Count a plan once by name and agent using the existing hub deduplication order. A plan contributes from its `since` month onward. Older records without `since` are active. Elapsed Plan fee equals the full Plan fee for a completed month and is proportional to elapsed UTC time for the current month.
- After three full days have elapsed in the current UTC month, Monthly pace is the recorded month-to-date API-equivalent plus the time left in the month multiplied by the sum of two daily means. For factory jobs use the latest seven days within the month, using the elapsed window while the month is younger than seven days. For Outside usage divide the month-to-date amount by elapsed days. Omit Monthly pace before the three-day minimum.
- Keep the standalone Plans behavior available with the existing Python response through a compatibility adapter.

**Key interfaces:**

- The hub monthly comparison module exposes month-keyed comparison rows so callers can select the same figures without re-aggregating jobs, plans or ready scans.
- `Hub::overview()` and `Hub::subscriptions()` expose the comparison rows their readers need. The hub overview and the hub-backed `planPage()` consume the figures supplied by that module.
- The reported subscriptions list carries optional `since` as `YYYY-MM` through `subscription_statements()` and hub reads. The standalone document remains supported by the compatibility adapter.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] A shared fixture with a $50 job with only $20 attributed to Claude and $10 of ready Claude Outside usage shows $60 API-equivalent in both views. The factory Agent split accounts for the remaining $30 as `other`. A total-only legacy job has its factory cost assigned to Claude.
- [ ] A two-machine fixture includes a retained job and both ready and scanning Outside usage. Both views agree on current and previous month totals and include only ready Outside usage under the keys supplied by 01. A job at November 1, 00:30 UTC is counted in November even with a browser time zone where it is still October.
- [ ] At November 16, 00:00 UTC, fixtures with two copies of a $120 plan without `since` and a future $80 plan show one active plan with a $120 full Plan fee and a $60 elapsed Plan fee. The $80 plan first contributes in its reported `since` month. Both views label the two fee values.
- [ ] At November 16, 00:00 UTC, a fixture with $100 in factory jobs this month, $70 of that in the last seven days, and $30 in Outside usage yields the same $310 Monthly pace in both views. The fixture checks that pace is absent before three full UTC days and available once three have elapsed.
- [ ] The hub overview and hub-backed Plans both read the monthly comparison without independently aggregating the underlying jobs, scans or plans. A standalone fixture checks that the Python response still renders Plans and its missing-configuration state.

**Out of scope:**

- Matching standalone and hub calculations or replacing the standalone Python path.
- Session ingestion, pricing and deduplication rules owned by ticket 01.
- Cost aggregation outside the subscription comparison, including the separate Costs view work in ticket 07.
